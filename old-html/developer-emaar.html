About Emaar Properties

Building the pulse of Dubai.

Emaar Properties is one of the world’s leading property developers, headquartered in the United Arab Emirates. With proven competencies in properties, shopping malls, retail, hospitality and leisure, Emaar offers a lifestyle focused on design excellence, build quality and timely delivery.

Building on the legacy of its flagship Downtown Dubai creations — the iconic Burj Khalifa, The Dubai Mall and Dubai Fountain — Emaar’s portfolio spans master-planned communities, premium malls and retail, and a hospitality and leisure portfolio that includes Address Hotels & Resorts and Armani Hotels & Resorts.
