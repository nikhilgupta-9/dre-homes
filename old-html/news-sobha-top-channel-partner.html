DRE Homes Recognised as a Top-Performing Channel Partner – Sobha Realty

DRE NewsroomAug 20, 20262 min read
Share

DRE Homes Real Estate is proud to announce its recognition by Sobha Realty as a Top-Performing Channel Partner for Q2 2026 at the Sobha Star H1 Award 2026.

While awards are often associated with performance and numbers, this recognition represents something much more meaningful for DRE Homes — the trust of clients, the strength of partnerships, and the dedication of a team committed to delivering excellence in Dubai’s luxury real estate market.

A Recognition Built on Performance and Trust

Achieving the position of a Top Performing Channel Partner is the result of consistent effort, market understanding, and a strong focus on client relationships.

Over the years, DRE Homes Real Estate has worked with a diverse global community of investors exploring opportunities in Dubai. Each client brings a different vision — from purchasing a luxury home to building a long-term property portfolio.

Understanding these individual goals and providing the right guidance has remained at the heart of DRE Homes’ approach. The recognition from Sobha Realty reflects this continued commitment to delivering a professional and client-focused real estate experience.

In our words

For DRE Homes, the award is not simply a celebration of past performance. It is motivation to continue setting higher standards and creating greater value for clients.

Celebrating the Sobha Star H1 Award 2026

The Sobha Star H1 Award 2026 marks an important moment for DRE Homes Real Estate.

Being recognised by Sobha Realty as a Top Performing Channel Partner – Q2 2026 highlights the strong relationship between the two organisations and the contribution of DRE Homes to Sobha Realty’s growing presence in Dubai’s premium property market.

Connecting a Global Community With Dubai

Dubai has become a leading destination for international property investors, offering a combination of luxury living, world-class infrastructure, business opportunities, and a dynamic real estate market.

As more investors from around the world explore Dubai property, the role of a trusted real estate advisor becomes increasingly important.

DRE Homes Real Estate aims to bridge that gap by helping clients understand the market, explore suitable opportunities, and make informed property decisions. The company's relationship with leading developers, including Sobha Realty, allows DRE Homes to connect its international clientele with some of Dubai’s most sought-after residential developments.

The People Behind the Achievement

Every company recognition has a team behind it.

From client consultations and property viewings to negotiations and after-sales support, every successful transaction is the result of teamwork and dedication.

The Sobha recognition is therefore a milestone for the entire DRE Homes family. It celebrates the people who work every day to turn client aspirations into successful real estate journeys.

Looking Ahead

The Top Performing Channel Partner – Q2 2026 recognition is another proud chapter in the DRE Homes Real Estate story.

But the journey does not stop with an award. As Dubai’s real estate market continues to evolve and attract investors from across the globe, DRE Homes remains focused on building stronger partnerships, delivering personalised advisory, and helping clients discover the right opportunities in Dubai’s luxury property market.

The Sobha Star H1 Award 2026 is a moment to celebrate — and a reminder to keep moving forward. 2026 has begun with recognition. The next chapter begins with even greater ambition.
