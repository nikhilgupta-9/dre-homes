Qurat Ul AinCo-Founder & CLO

DRE Homes Real Estate was established in 2007 as a boutique brokerage firm to fill in the service void that then existed in Dubai's booming property market. A number of brokerages were offering “plain vanilla” services of buying and selling properties. But we wanted to be different and set a benchmark.

As the Dubai real estate market passed through its natural ebb and flow, the need for experts who could guide investors to adopt long-term investment strategies became the need of the hour. We at DRE distinguished ourselves in that area by persistently advising investors on investment and acquisition strategies, asset management, and successful exit strategies.

We feel proud to say that we were blessed with clients who have believed in us and continue to work with us. This stands as a rock-solid testimony to our capabilities. We look forward to building new relationships and are eager to hear from you.
